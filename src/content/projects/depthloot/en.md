A series of shortform vertical video ads produced for the social media marketing of DepthLoot, an upcoming indie game currently in development for release on [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4637840/), across TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts. I was responsible for the end-to-end creative direction: conceptualizing hooks and video ideas, copywriting descriptions, editing with retention-driven pacing, and driving the overall acquisition strategy.
