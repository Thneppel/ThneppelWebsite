Privacy policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This policy explains what personal data is handled on thneppel.com.br (including the Portuguese version, the blog and landing pages such as the real estate media page), what it is used for and what your rights are, under Brazil’s General Data Protection Law (Law No. 13,709/2018, LGPD).

In short: the site sets no cookies of its own and has no analytics or advertising tools. Personal data is only handled when you send a message through a form or interact with a YouTube video.

1. Who is responsible

The controller of the data is Thiago Neppel, an individual who runs this website. For anything related to privacy, write to contact@thneppel.com.br.

2. What data is handled

Contact forms. When you send a message, the site collects what you fill in:

  • on the home page: name, email and message;
  • on the real estate media page: name, email, phone, company and message.

Technical access data. As with any website, the hosting service automatically logs data such as IP address, browser, operating system, page visited and date and time of access.

YouTube videos. Portfolio videos are shown with the YouTube player in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). When you hover over a thumbnail or open a video, the player loads and Google may collect data such as your IP address and device information. To make videos open faster, the page also connects to YouTube’s servers as soon as it loads, which exposes your IP address to those servers even if you don’t watch any video.

3. What the data is used for

Purpose Data Legal basis (LGPD)
Replying to messages and sending quotes Form data Pre-contractual steps at your request (art. 7, V), or legitimate interest (art. 7, IX)
Keeping the site online, secure and working Technical access data Legitimate interest (art. 7, IX)
Showing portfolio videos Data collected by YouTube Legitimate interest (art. 7, IX)

Data is not used for advertising, is never sold and is not subject to automated decision-making.

4. Who the data is shared with

Data only goes through the services needed to run the site:

  • Web3Forms: receives the form and delivers it by email;
  • GitHub (GitHub Pages): hosts the site and logs technical access data;
  • Google (YouTube): plays the embedded videos;
  • Proton (Proton Mail): the email service used to receive and answer messages.

Beyond these, data is only shared when required by law or court order.

5. International transfers

The services above may store and process data outside Brazil, mainly in the United States. These transfers are made to do what you asked for (such as replying to a message) and with providers that apply data protection safeguards, as allowed by art. 33 of the LGPD.

6. Cookies

This site sets no cookies of its own and does not use browser storage to track you. The YouTube player, once loaded, may use Google’s own cookies and similar technologies, under Google’s privacy policy.

7. How long data is kept

  • Form messages: for as long as needed to reply and follow up on the conversation or project, and then for any period required by law (for example, for tax purposes when a service is hired). You can ask for deletion at any time.
  • Technical access data: for the period set by GitHub in its privacy policy.

8. Security

The site is served only over a secure connection (HTTPS), and the forms are protected against automated spam. No system is completely immune to failures, but reasonable measures are taken to protect your information.

9. Your rights

Under the LGPD (art. 18), you can at any time:

  • confirm whether your data is processed and access it;
  • correct incomplete, inaccurate or outdated data;
  • request anonymization, blocking or deletion of unnecessary data or data handled in breach of the law;
  • request data portability;
  • find out who your data has been shared with;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest.

To exercise any of these rights, write to contact@thneppel.com.br. Requests are answered within 15 days. You can also file a complaint with Brazil’s National Data Protection Authority (ANPD).

10. Children

This site is aimed at professionals and companies and does not knowingly collect data from children or teenagers.

11. Links to other sites

The site links to other services such as Instagram, X, Discord, YouTube, Steam, MySoundLib and client websites. Each has its own privacy policy, which this document does not cover.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the site changes. The date of the last update is shown at the top of the page.
