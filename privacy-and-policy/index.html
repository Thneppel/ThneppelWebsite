Privacy policy

This policy explains how personal data is handled on this portfolio website under Brazilian law, including the General Data Protection Law (LGPD).

1. Applicable law

This website complies with the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados, LGPD).

2. Data collection

This website may collect:

  • Contact details such as name and email address when submitted through a form
  • Technical connection data, including IP address and browser logs generated automatically by hosting infrastructure

3. Purpose

Personal data is used only to:

  • Reply to direct inquiries
  • Maintain site stability and performance

4. Cookies

This website does not use tracking cookies. Any analytics tools in place are limited to non-invasive traffic measurement.

5. Data sharing

Personal data is not shared with third parties, except when required by applicable law or judicial order.

6. Retention and security

Information is retained only as long as necessary to answer inquiries or maintain the site, protected by standard technical safeguards.

7. User rights

Under Brazilian law, you may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information at any time.

8. Contact

For questions regarding this policy or personal data, write to: contact@thneppel.com.br
